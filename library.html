Available in screening

Choose the topic shown below after entering your age group.

  • Mood self-report PHQ-9; Bipolar Inventory of Signs and Symptoms Scale (BISS); Depression Anxiety Stress Scales (DASS-21); Altman Self-Rating Mania Scale (ASRM); Quick Inventory of Depressive Symptomatology (QIDS-SR16); Mood Disorder Questionnaire (MDQ); Hypomania Checklist (HCL-32); Beck Depression Inventory-II (BDI-II). The K10 is available separately under Psychological well-being.
  • Anxiety self-report GAD-7; Beck Anxiety Inventory (BAI); Penn State Worry Questionnaire (PSWQ); Panic Disorder Severity Scale Self-Report (PDSS-SR); Mobility Inventory for Agoraphobia (MIA); Liebowitz Social Anxiety Scale (LSAS); Brief Fear of Negative Evaluation Scale (BFNE); Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale (HADS); MCQ-30; SPIN; TCQ; IUS-27; SCARED child and parent forms (ages 8–17). Select these under the Anxiety journey; the app administers one chosen form at a time.
  • Trauma self-report PCL-5; Impact of Event Scale–Revised (IES-R); Acute Stress Disorder Scale (ASDS) supplied COVID-19-framed version; International Trauma Questionnaire (ITQ); Adjustment Disorder – New Module (ADNM-20). Select a self-report form from the Trauma journey. These scores describe symptoms and do not establish a diagnosis.
  • Clinician trauma interview CAPS-5 Past Month is selectable as a clinician-only scoring worksheet under Trauma. It requires formal structured-interview training and the official interview prompts. The app reports symptom-cluster totals and a criteria tally for clinician review, not an autonomous diagnosis.
  • Dissociation Multidimensional Inventory of Dissociation — MID-60 (short form); Dissociative Experiences Scale — DES-II
  • Sleep ISI and Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index (PSQI), selectable in the Sleep journey.
  • Substance Use AUDITC; Drinking Motives Questionnaire – Revised (DMQ-R); Alcohol Abstinence Self-Efficacy Scale (AASE), confidence and temptation forms
  • Difficulty focusing & Attention ASRS-v1.1 (adult); Vanderbilt parent form (younger ages)
  • Somatic symptoms & health-related preoccupation PHQ-15 — Somatic Symptom Severity Scale; FAC22-SR — Factitious Disorder Symptom Scale (Self-Report)
  • Thoughts & Perceptions CAARMS; Revised Green et al. Paranoid Thoughts Scale (R-GPTS); Aberrant Salience Inventory (ASI); PANSS and PANSS-6; BPRS-24; and PSYRATS. The clinician-rated forms are selectable under this journey.
  • Functioning & cross-cutting symptoms MINI Optional Assessment A — Cross-Cutting Measures: Severity of Symptom; MINI Optional Assessment B — Disability / Functional Impairment: Severity of Disability / Impairment
  • Personality functioning IPDE ICD-10 Module Screening Questionnaire; PDQ-4+ Personality Questionnaire (99 items); HEXACO-PI-R (Observer Report Form); McLean Screening Instrument for BPD (MSI-BPD); Five Factor Obsessive-Compulsive Inventory (FFOCI-SF); IPIP-NEO-120 (Johnson)
  • Anger and impulse control STAXI-CA — State-Trait Anger Expression Inventory for Children and Adolescents; Clinical Anger Scale (CAS)
  • Gender identity, incongruence & related distress Gender Congruence and Life Satisfaction Scale (GCLS); UGDS-GS
  • Psychological well-being Ryff Scales of Psychological Well-Being (84 items); Mindful Attention Awareness Scale (MAAS); Difficulties in Emotion Regulation Scale (DERS); Emotional Expressivity Scale (EES); Schutte Self-Report Emotional Intelligence Test (SSEIT); Acceptance and Action Questionnaire (AAQ-II); Flourishing Scale (FS); Valuing Questionnaire (VQ); Cognitive Flexibility Inventory (CFI); Distress Tolerance Scale (DTS)
  • Suicide safety follow-up (with clinician) C-SSRS · Since last contact

Additional self-report and informant measures

These references identify additional forms in the collection. Their responses are outside the screening summary.

  • Adjustment and family life McMaster Family Assessment Device (FAD).
  • Emotion and coping Compassion Motivation and Action Scales (self-compassion); Anxiety Control Questionnaire (ACQ).
  • Obsessive-compulsive symptoms OCI-R and DOCS self-report forms and clinician-rated Y-BOCS are selectable in the Obsessions and Compulsions journey; each is scored by its published subscales and total.
  • Eating and body image Eating Disorder Examination Questionnaire (EDE-Q 6.0), a 28-day self-report measure. Select it in the Eating and Body Image journey.
  • Clinician-rated anxiety Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale (HAM-A), available as a clinician-rated choice in the Anxiety journey. The Panic Attack Questionnaire in the supplied packet is an interview/history form, not a scored questionnaire in this app; use it as a clinician reference.
  • Alcohol, cannabis and tobacco Obsessive Compulsive Drinking Scale (OCDS); Marijuana Problems Inventory (MPI); marijuana attitudes and use form (MDQ); Wisconsin Smoking Withdrawal Scale (WSWS).
  • Child and family Child Obsessive-Compulsive Impact Scale - Revised, child form (COIS-RC) is selectable under Obsessions and Compulsions. Family Accommodation Scale for OCD and Vanderbilt teacher/follow-up forms remain references.
  • General psychological distress Kessler Psychological Distress Scale (K10), available as an optional 10-item form under Psychological well-being. It gives a broad distress score and is not diagnostic; use population-appropriate interpretation.
  • Personality Borderline Personality Questionnaire (BPQ); Five Factor Model Rating Form (FFMRF); Big Five Inventory (BFI); HEXACO-60 observer form; Maladaptive Schema Scale (MSS v1.4).
  • DSM personality measures PID-5-BF Adult is selectable as a self-report form with five trait-domain scores. PID-5-SF and full PID-5 Adult remain references. APA assessment measures
  • Functioning Sheehan Disability Scale (SDS); WHODAS 2.0 forms and manual.

Clinician interviews and ratings

Use the appropriate edition, interview instructions and training for the setting.

  • Psychosis and movement PANSS, PANSS-6, and BPRS-24 are selectable clinician-rated options under Thoughts & Perceptions. Barnes Akathisia Rating Scale (BARS) is selectable under Movement Symptoms. Abnormal Involuntary Movement Scale (AIMS) remains a reference.
  • Clinician-rated mood forms HDRS-17/HAM-D-17, MADRS, YMRS and BDRS are selectable in the Mood journey. They require clinician administration and use of the correct item anchors/version. HDRS17, MADRS and BDRS include suicide-related items; endorsements activate the app safety support pathway. These severity measures do not diagnose.
  • Obsessions and compulsions OCI-R and DOCS self-report; clinician-rated Y-BOCS, Y-BOCS-II, CY-BOCS, and adult BDD-YBOCS scoring forms; and child self-report COIS-RC impact measure.
  • Substance withdrawal CIWA-Ar and COWS are selectable clinician-rated observation forms under Substance Use.
  • Diagnostic interviews MINI 7.0.2 modules; Standardised Assessment of Personality - Abbreviated Scale (SAPAS); SCID-5-PD structured personality interview. The Personality journey includes an interactive clinician summary-score worksheet; administer the full interview using its authorized materials.
  • Suicide safety SAFE-T clinician guide; C-SSRS versions for baseline and follow-up.
  • Specialist assessment Forensic symptom and performance validity assessment, including the attachment’s discussion of SIRS, MMPI, PAI and performance validity tests; Goodenough–Harris drawing test, Draw-a-Person and Sacks Sentence Completion Test (SSCT).

Publisher materials and controlled tests

Use the publisher’s materials for administration. Item booklets, norm tables and scoring keys are omitted here.

  • Children and schools Adjustment Inventory for School Students (AISS); Behavior Rating Inventory of Executive Function – Adult Version (BRIEF-A, adult self-report); Achenbach Child Behavior Checklist (CBCL) and related youth reports.
  • Mood, anger and anxiety Beck Depression Inventory-II (BDI-II); Beck Anxiety Inventory (BAI); State–Trait Anxiety Inventory (STAI); State–Trait Anger Expression Inventory-2 (STAXI-2); Sinha’s Comprehensive Anxiety Test (SCAT).
  • Family and self-concept Family Pathology Scale (FPS-VVDA); Self-Concept Questionnaire (SCQ).
  • Personality and symptoms NEO Five-Factor Inventory (NEO-FFI); short Eysenck Personality Questionnaire Revised (EPQ-R); Maudsley Personality Inventory (MPI); Symptom Checklist-90 (SCL-90 family).
  • Cognition and structured assessment Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA) forms; SCID-5-PD interview materials. Obtain the authorized version and scoring materials from the publisher.
