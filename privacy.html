Privacy Policy

EPCSS has no personal data to protect because it never collects any. Privacy here is an architectural property, not a promise.

  • Zero collection We do not ask for your name, contact details, government ID, location, or any identifying information. The screening collects only symptom responses and coarse context (age group, reason for visit).
  • Zero transmission There is no backend, no API, no analytics, and no telemetry. Your browser only ever downloads static files. A strict Content Security Policy (connect-src 'self') blocks any cross-origin data requests.
  • Zero persistence Responses live in volatile memory only. localStorage, sessionStorage, cookies, IndexedDB, and service workers are not used. Closing the tab destroys all clinical data irreversibly.
  • Your exports are yours The PDF report is generated entirely on your device. Where you save or share it is entirely your choice; we never see it.
  • No tracking No third-party scripts other than the vetted jsPDF CDN libraries used solely for offline-capable PDF generation, which receive no user data.
  • Data subject rights Because nothing is stored anywhere, there is nothing for us to access, correct, disclose, or delete. You can verify this with your browser's network panel.
  • Crisis resources Safety-related information shown by the app is static, localized text; contacting a helpline is done by you, directly, and is invisible to this application.
  • Changes Any change to this architecture would require a visible code change in the public repository, auditable via version control.
If you use a shared or public computer, close the tab after finishing your session to destroy all answers from memory.
