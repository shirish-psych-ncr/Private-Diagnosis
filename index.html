About Serenity Clinic

For over 15 years, Serenity Mental Health Clinic has been providing compassionate, evidence-based mental health care to individuals, couples, and families in our community.

Our mission is to create a safe, welcoming environment where everyone feels comfortable seeking the help they deserve. We believe that mental health is just as important as physical health, and everyone deserves access to quality mental health care.
